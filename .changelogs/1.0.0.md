# NCapes 1.0.0

NCapes adds custom capes to Minecraft Forge 1.7.10 servers.

Press Ctrl+K to open the cape menu. Choose a PNG to upload it, or use "Remove cape" to clear your current cape. Clients running lwjgl3ify can also drop a PNG onto the open game window.

The server saves capes by player UUID and sends them to players when they join. NCapes is optional on either side of a connection: clients without it can join an NCapes server, and clients with it can join servers without it. Uploading and viewing NCapes capes requires the mod on both sides.

Cape PNGs must be 64x32, 128x64, 192x96, or 256x128 pixels and no larger than 24 KiB.

Clients need Controlling 2.1.7 or newer and Modular UI 2 with its dependencies. Dedicated servers only need NCapes. Install the main jar rather than the `-dev` or `-sources` jars.
